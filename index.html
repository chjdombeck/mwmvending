What we do

One partner for the whole break room

Pick what fits your space and headcount. We handle delivery, install, stocking, and service. You just tell us where it goes.

Vending machines

Snacks, cold drinks, and combo machines with tap-to-pay readers. Healthy options on request.

AI smart coolers

Grab-and-go coolers with AI checkout. Tap to unlock, take what you want, and walk out. Live stock data keeps them full.

Office coffee

Bean-to-cup brewers, single-serve, and the supplies to keep them running: coffee, tea, and everything alongside.

Micro-markets

A self-checkout mini-store for higher-traffic locations, with fresh food, wider selection, and open shelves.
